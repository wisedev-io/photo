# DV Photo Camera Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

DV Photo Camera (app.dvphoto.camera) is published by EDDE FOOT. Contact us about privacy at eddefoot@gmail.com.

## Photos, camera and storage

The app uses the camera when you grant permission or reads pictures you select in the system photo picker. It processes images and runs face positioning and photo checks on your device. The app does not upload photos or facial images to EDDE FOOT or Google. It does not require an account, show ads, or sell personal data.

When you save an image, the app writes the JPEG to the destination you choose. It keeps app preferences and a local history containing information about exported photos, such as their names and storage locations. Android may request storage permission on older devices to save files. The app disables its own Android backup.

You can delete exported images from their storage location using your device’s gallery or file manager. Clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app to remove its local preferences and history. Photos already exported to a shared or user-selected destination can remain after uninstalling. If you choose a cloud-backed destination, that provider handles the file under its own privacy terms.

## Google ML Kit diagnostics

The bundled Google ML Kit face detection SDK processes photos on the device. Google’s SDK may transmit diagnostics and usage metrics to Google over HTTPS. These can include device and app information, an installation identifier, feature events, image format or dimensions, performance metrics and error codes. Google describes this data as used for diagnostics and usage analytics. See [Google ML Kit’s data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure) for details. EDDE FOOT does not maintain a server-side copy of your photos or a developer-controlled diagnostics history.

Contact eddefoot@gmail.com with questions or requests about this policy.
